Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 16, 2026  ·  Last updated: May 16, 2026

The short version: SpeakFlowAI is a local-first app. Your voice recordings and coaching sessions are stored on your device and never uploaded to any server by default. We collect minimal analytics and crash data to improve the app. Premium AI analysis requires sending audio data to Anthropic's servers — this is always opt-in.

1. Who We Are

SpeakFlowAI ("we", "us", or "our") is a private leadership communication coaching application developed by Gyan Prakash. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at ngyan.prakash@gmail.com.

3. Voice Recordings & Local Analysis

Your voice recordings are the core of the app. By default:

  • Recordings are stored exclusively on your device using local storage.
  • All speech analysis (pace, pauses, clarity scoring) runs locally on your device using on-device processing.
  • Recordings are never uploaded to our servers on the free tier.
  • You can delete any recording or your entire history at any time from within the app.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all local recordings and coaching data.

4. Premium AI Analysis

SpeakFlowAI offers an optional premium subscription that provides advanced AI-powered coaching analysis. When you use this feature:

  • Audio data from your session is sent securely (HTTPS/TLS) to Anthropic's API for analysis.
  • Anthropic processes this data to generate coaching insights and does not retain audio beyond the API call.
  • This feature is opt-in — free tier users never have audio transmitted externally.
  • You can review Anthropic's privacy policy at anthropic.com/privacy.

5. Analytics — PostHog

We use PostHog to understand how the app is used so we can improve it. PostHog collects:

  • Feature usage events (e.g., recording started, analysis viewed)
  • Session duration and app open frequency
  • Device type, OS version, and app version

PostHog data is anonymized — we do not link analytics events to your identity. Data is stored on PostHog's EU-based servers. You can review their policy at posthog.com/privacy.

6. Crash Reporting — Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics (by Google) to receive automatic crash reports. When the app crashes, it sends:

  • Stack trace and error details
  • Device model, Android version, and app version
  • No personal data or voice content is ever included in crash reports.

Review Google's privacy policy at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

7. Subscriptions — RevenueCat

Premium subscriptions are managed through RevenueCat and the Google Play billing system. RevenueCat may collect:

  • Subscription status and purchase history
  • A pseudonymous user identifier

We do not receive or store your payment card details — all payment processing is handled by Google Play. Review RevenueCat's policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

8. Data Retention

  • Local data (recordings, analyses, reflections): retained until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • Analytics data (PostHog): retained for 12 months, then automatically deleted.
  • Crash reports (Crashlytics): retained for 90 days.
  • Subscription data (RevenueCat): retained per RevenueCat's data retention policy.

9. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Delete local data: Use the app's settings to delete individual sessions or all coaching history. Uninstalling the app removes all local data.
  • Request analytics deletion: Email us at ngyan.prakash@gmail.com to request deletion of your PostHog analytics data.
  • Opt out of analytics: Contact us and we will disable analytics collection for your device.
  • Access your data: All your coaching data is directly accessible within the app.

10. Data Security

  • All data transmitted to third-party services uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Local data is stored in the app's private storage directory, inaccessible to other apps.
  • We do not sell your data to any third party.
  • We do not use your data for advertising purposes.

11. Children's Privacy

SpeakFlowAI is designed for adults (18+), specifically founders and executives. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We will notify users of significant changes through an in-app notice. The effective date at the top of this page reflects when the policy was last updated.
